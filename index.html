🩺 Physical Exam Guide

OSCE Checklist & Heart/Lung Audio Library

📌 WIPER Protocol (Before Starting)

  • Wash hands & sanitize.
  • Introduce yourself & state role.
  • Permission & Privacy.
  • Expose & Position appropriately.
  • Right side (stand on patient's right side).

🔬 Core Steps (IPPA)

  • 1. Inspection 👁️: General state, skin (pallor/jaundice/cyanosis), hands & clubbing.
  • 2. Palpation ✋: Temp, tenderness, organomegaly, pulse rate & rhythm.
  • 3. Percussion 👆: Resonant (lungs) vs Dull (liver/fluid).
  • 4. Auscultation 🎧: Heart sounds ($S_1, S_2$), breath sounds, bowel sounds.
